# Data use

The datasets in this repository are provided for research evaluation.

Treasury, TIPS, breakeven, fed funds and mortgage series are from FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis). Social post counts are from LunarCrush.

Contact [joe@lunarcrush.com](mailto:joe@lunarcrush.com) for commercial licensing, redistribution, hourly data, point-in-time history, or a version cut to a custom topic or ticker universe.

This dataset and the accompanying analysis do not constitute investment advice.
